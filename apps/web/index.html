Mission

Create a strong community for burn survivors.

Rekindle & Rise helps survivors feel less alone by bringing lived experience, trusted support, and everyday connection into one respectful space.

  • Connect with moderated peer communities.
  • Reflect privately and at your own pace.
  • Find local and virtual events with clear details.

Caregivers, families, volunteers, and community allies are welcome too.

FAQ

Quick answers

Who is Rekindle & Rise for?

Survivors, caregivers, volunteers, and community allies seeking structured, respectful support online.

Do I need to share my journal entries?

No. Journal entries are private by default and can remain private permanently.

Can I explore events before sharing much profile information?

Yes. You can browse community and event details while keeping profile visibility limited.
