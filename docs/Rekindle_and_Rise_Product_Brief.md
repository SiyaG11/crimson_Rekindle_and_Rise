# Rekindle & Rise App — Product Brief

## Purpose
The Rekindle & Rise app is a survivor-centered community platform designed to help burn survivors and their supporters find connection, learn from trusted resources, and privately document healing progress.

The app supports:
- finding supportive events and verified organizations
- connecting with other survivors in moderated community spaces
- participating in location-based and interest-based forums
- privately journaling the healing journey
- discovering activities, prompts, and reflective exercises
- controlling how much personal and burn-related information is shared

The app is explicitly positioned as a peer-support platform, not a substitute for professional medical or mental-health care.

## Primary Users
- **Burn Survivor** — the main user. Can join groups, attend events, read survivor stories, and keep a private healing journal.
- **Caregiver or Family Member** — access educational resources, events, and designated caregiver communities while preserving survivor privacy.
- **Burn-Support Initiative / Approved Organization** — publish events, volunteer opportunities, educational posts, and initiative updates.
- **Moderator / Administrator** — approve groups and organizations, moderate content, respond to reports, manage events, and suspend unsafe accounts.

## Key User Journeys
### Onboarding
1. Welcome screen with mission, community introduction, and clear peer-support framing.
2. Select user type: survivor, caregiver/family, volunteer/supporter, or organization representative.
3. Confirm age and consent; support independent accounts for ages 13+, with a legal review needed before under-13 accounts launch.
4. Create account with required fields: display name, email, password, age confirmation, and general location.
5. Privacy setup before asking for sensitive burn information: default privacy, per-field visibility controls, and explanation of how information is shared.
6. Optional healing profile: recovery stage, injury year, support topics, event interests, similarity preferences, and accessibility needs.
7. Select interests across emotional support, recovery, volunteering, creative activities, events, and education.
8. Review community guidelines and reporting controls before entering the app.

### Home Dashboard
A personalized landing page showing:
- a welcome card with a supportive message and quick journal access
- upcoming local and virtual events, including verified organization events
- community posts such as survivor stories, questions, milestones, and encouraging content
- initiative updates like care-kit drives, workshops, volunteer opportunities, and fundraising campaigns
- quick actions for journaling, finding events, joining groups, and creating posts

### Community
- Safe group-based conversations organized by location and topic.
- Groups for regional survivor communities, caregiver/family support, return-to-school/work, confidence and body image, and prevention volunteers.
- Features include discussion posts, group chat, comments, reactions, polls, pinned resources, event announcements, and anonymous posting where appropriate.
- Strong safety controls: moderation of sensitive content, graphic warnings with hidden previews, account blocks/mutes, and limited posting privileges for new accounts.
- Direct messaging is only considered after group moderation and reporting systems are established.

### Healing Journal
- Private by default; entries do not appear in feeds or community groups automatically.
- Supports free writing, guided prompts, mood check-ins, gratitude entries, recovery milestones, goals, photo attachments, voice-to-text, calendar history, and search.
- Users can download entries, delete individual entries or their whole journal, control backups, and share single entries deliberately.
- Example prompts center on strengths, progress, support, comfort, goals, and personal meaning of healing.

### Profile and Account
- Public or community profile elements: display name, photo, general location, bio, interests, joined groups, and optional survivor story.
- Private account info only when necessary: legal name, email, password, age verification, notifications, and security settings.
- Optional burn-related profile data is private by default and includes injury year, recovery stage, support interests, accessibility needs, and topics they are comfortable discussing.
- Privacy controls let users set each field to: only me, my groups, all registered members, or public where appropriate.

## Privacy and Safety Principles
- Sensitive data should be optional and private by default.
- The app should not claim HIPAA compliance solely because it uses encryption or stores health-related data.
- HIPAA applicability depends on whether the app operates for a covered entity or business associate.
- If hospitals or providers send protected health information, the app may need Business Associate Agreements and compliance with privacy, security, and breach notification rules.
- The FTC Health Breach Notification Rule may apply to consumer health apps regardless of HIPAA.
- For younger survivors, under-13 accounts must not launch without COPPA-compliant parental-consent and data handling.
- Before development, the organization must decide whether the platform operates independently or with hospitals, whether providers send patient data, which vendors store content, and how users can access, export, correct, or delete their information.
- A privacy attorney and qualified security professional should review the architecture before handling sensitive health information.

## Roadmap at a Glance
1. Discover Rekindle & Rise
2. Choose user type and create an account
3. Set privacy and communication preferences
4. Optionally create a healing profile
5. Enter a personalized home page
6. Browse events, survivor posts, and initiatives
7. Join moderated location or interest groups
8. Use the private healing journal
9. Build trusted community connections
10. Participate in events, volunteering, and recovery activities

## What This App Needs to Deliver First
- A trust-building onboarding experience that explains mission, privacy, and peer-support purpose.
- A privacy-first account and profile model with clear controls over sensitive information.
- Community spaces that are moderated, location-aware, and supportive.
- A private journaling experience with optional prompts and export/delete controls.
- A home dashboard that surfaces events, updates, and personal actions relevant to the user.

## Notes / Assumptions
- The app is described as a web or mobile community platform; the brief uses neutral language for both.
- The product is meant to support survivors and their wider support network while prioritizing privacy, safety, and moderated connection.
- Further product definition should clarify hospital partnerships, exact user roles, and the initial minimum viable feature set.
