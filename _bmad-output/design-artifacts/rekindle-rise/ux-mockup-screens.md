# Rekindle & Rise UX Mockup Screens

## Purpose
These mockups define a more comprehensive, professional first-release experience for Rekindle & Rise as a calm, privacy-first, trauma-informed community platform. The screens now reflect richer UI patterns such as calendar-based event discovery, map-assisted location awareness, and more polished dashboard and journaling flows.

## Design direction
- Visual tone: warm, grounded, reassuring, and credible
- Layout: spacious, low-friction, mobile-first, with strong responsive web support
- Interaction style: clear hierarchy, visible trust signals, and calm, guided actions
- Content approach: supportive and plain-language, never clinical or overly promotional

## Core screens

### 1. Welcome / Landing
Purpose: Introduce the mission, explain the app’s peer-support focus, and guide users toward account creation or sign-in.

Layout:
- A refined hero section with a stronger brand message and clear primary CTA
- Supporting panels for privacy, moderation, and optional journaling
- Secondary actions for sign-in and guest exploration

Professional notes:
- Use a polished hero treatment with space, warmth, and confidence.
- Emphasize the community promise without feeling overly commercial.

---

### 2. Role Selection and Onboarding
Purpose: Help users create an account and choose the role that best fits their experience.

Layout:
- Card-based role selection with supportive labels and visual separation
- Consent and safety reminders in a concise, reassuring format
- Strong progress indication and an obvious continue action

Professional notes:
- Clear role choices should feel simple and non-judgmental.
- The onboarding experience should signal safety and trust from the first screen.

---

### 3. Privacy Setup
Purpose: Explain privacy choices early and make safe defaults obvious.

Layout:
- A dedicated privacy card or section with clear controls for visibility and sharing
- Plain-language descriptions for profile, journal, and story settings
- Strong visual treatment for safe default states

Professional notes:
- Privacy should feel empowering rather than technical.
- The controls should be easily readable and easy to adjust later.

---

### 4. Home Dashboard
Purpose: Deliver a welcoming first experience with relevant actions, community updates, and gentle prompts.

Layout:
- A polished dashboard with a welcome banner, quick actions, and reminder cards
- Summary widgets for upcoming events, joined groups, and recent journal activity
- Navigation that balances community content with private reflection actions

Professional notes:
- The dashboard should feel calm and useful rather than busy.
- Encourage journaling and community discovery without overwhelming the user.

---

### 5. Community Groups
Purpose: Let users discover safe, relevant groups that match their needs, interests, or location.

Layout:
- Search and filter controls alongside curated group cards
- Visible moderation and safety status for each group
- Clear actions to view details or join

Professional notes:
- Group discovery should feel welcoming and trustworthy.
- Use moderation and verification cues to reinforce safety.

---

### 6. Group Discussion Thread
Purpose: Support conversations in a way that feels welcoming, readable, and safe.

Layout:
- A structured feed with post cards, reactions, and moderation affordances
- A comment composer that feels lightweight and unobtrusive
- Optional pinned resources or event callouts

Professional notes:
- The discussion experience should feel calm and relatively low-noise.
- Safety tools must stay visible but not dominant.

---

### 7. Healing Journal Entry
Purpose: Encourage private reflection in a low-pressure, emotionally safe environment.

Layout:
- A focused journaling workspace with prompt or free-writing mode
- Supportive affordances for voice-to-text, attachment, and save actions
- A privacy indicator and clear options for later review

Professional notes:
- The layout should feel quiet, minimal, and emotionally grounding.
- Prompts should be gentle and empowering rather than overly directive.

---

### 8. Journal History
Purpose: Give users a simple way to revisit past entries, search, and manage their journal.

Layout:
- Entry cards with date, mood, and summary information
- Filtering and search affordances for later retrieval
- Clear separation between reading, editing, and deletion actions

Professional notes:
- The experience should support both reflection and long-term self-tracking.
- Keep the interaction simple and emotionally comfortable.

---

### 9. Event Discovery
Purpose: Help users explore local or virtual events that are relevant, trustworthy, and easy to join.

Layout:
- A combined experience using a calendar view, location-aware map, and event list
- Filter chips for date, location, and topic
- Event cards with host, accessibility, and RSVP details

Professional notes:
- The calendar and map should feel like first-class components, not decorative extras.
- Event discovery should communicate trust through verified organization badges and clear safety cues.

---

### 10. Event Detail
Purpose: Give users confidence and clarity before they RSVP or save an event.

Layout:
- Event header with title, date, location, and organizer
- Supporting details such as accessibility, format, and cost
- Clear call to action for RSVP or save

Professional notes:
- The detail view should feel calm and informative, not pushy or promotional.
- Secondary actions such as reminders and share should remain understated.

---

### 11. Profile and Settings
Purpose: Let users manage their public presence, privacy controls, and support preferences without feeling exposed.

Layout:
- Profile header with basic identity and role information
- Grouped privacy and notification settings with plain-language labels
- A clean save-and-update experience

Professional notes:
- Public-facing details should remain intentionally lightweight.
- The profile experience should feel controlled, personal, and reassuring.

---

## Screen priority for MVP
1. Welcome / Landing
2. Role Selection and Onboarding
3. Privacy Setup
4. Home Dashboard
5. Healing Journal Entry
6. Community Groups
7. Event Discovery

## Notes for implementation
- The visual system should stay consistent across mobile and web.
- The experience should support both first-time users and returning users without requiring them to re-learn the interface.
- Every screen should be reviewed for accessibility, privacy clarity, emotional tone, and visual polish.
