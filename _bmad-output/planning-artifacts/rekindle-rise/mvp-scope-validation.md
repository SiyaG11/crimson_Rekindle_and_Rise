# Rekindle & Rise MVP Scope Validation

## Purpose
This checklist is intended for leadership review before implementation begins. It helps confirm that the MVP scope is focused, feasible, and aligned with the product mission.

## Leadership Review Decisions

### 1. Mission and value proposition
- Central mission confirmed: create a safe, supportive community for burn survivors and their supporters.
- MVP priority confirmed: connection, belonging, and peer support should be the core focus over broader social features.

### 2. MVP scope decisions
- Core MVP experiences confirmed for launch: onboarding, privacy controls, community groups, journal, and event discovery.
- Real-time messaging is now considered part of the MVP experience and should not remain out of scope for v1.
- Launch approach confirmed: a hybrid web-plus-mobile experience is preferred.

### 3. User roles and access
- v1 roles confirmed: survivor/participant, supporter/caregiver, organization representative, moderator, and administrator.
- Peer mentor and professional partner roles should wait for later phases, as they require more governance, onboarding, and trust infrastructure than the initial release can safely support.

### 4. Safety and governance
- Parent-managed under-13 access is accepted as the policy for minors.
- Organization verification and moderator approval remain appropriate for launch readiness.

### 5. Launch readiness
- Must-have features for launch: journal with interactive prompts and missions, event calendar based on location, community forums with real-time chat, posting inspirational stories and recovery journeys to followers, and friend/follow capabilities.
- Minimum evidence of readiness for a first release should include: successful end-to-end testing of onboarding, privacy controls, community posting, real-time chat, journal workflows, and event discovery; validated moderation and reporting workflows; privacy review for minors and health-related data; and a clear support and escalation plan for launch.

## Suggested MVP Priorities

### Must-have for launch
- Account creation and onboarding
- Privacy and consent controls
- Community groups, discussion workflows, and real-time chat
- Private journal with prompts and missions
- Event calendar with location-based discovery and RSVP
- Social connection features such as following others and sharing stories
- Moderation and reporting

### Defer to later phases
- Advanced recommendations and discovery intelligence
- Full mobile-native app development
- Complex professional integrations
- Expanded governance roles such as peer mentor or professional partner
